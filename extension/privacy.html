Chrome extension · Version 1.1.0

HowConnected extension privacy

The extension uses the current tab only after you click its toolbar icon. It does not monitor browsing in the background, read browsing history, or store a history of the pages you visit.

Information used

  • The URL of the active tab is inspected locally to confirm it is an English Wikipedia article.
  • The article title is sent to English Wikipedia’s structured API to retrieve its Wikidata identifier and detect disambiguation pages.
  • Search text you type is sent to HowConnected’s entity-search endpoint.
  • The two selected Wikidata identifiers are placed in the HowConnected URL opened in a new tab.
  • Opened HowConnected pages include the fixed source=extension label for privacy-conscious referral measurement.

Information not collected

The extension does not transmit article content, collect browsing history, access cookies, save personal data, run analytics inside the extension, create advertising profiles, or use browsing activity for advertising. It has no background service worker or content script and makes no requests while the popup is closed.

Retention and sharing

The extension itself stores no user data. Requests are made only to English Wikipedia and HowConnected to provide the feature you requested. HowConnected does not receive the full active-tab URL or article text from the extension.

Limited use

Information accessed by this extension is used only to provide its single, user-initiated connection-finding feature. It is not sold, transferred for advertising, or used for personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising. HowConnected's use of information received from Chrome APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
